## About Me

I am Amit Kumar Ghosh, a Software Engineer, System Design Instructor at Scaler, and technical educator with over 6 years of experience building software products and mentoring engineers.

I specialize in Backend Engineering, Distributed Systems, System Design, Data Structures and Algorithms, and Software Architecture.

Alongside software development, I have trained and mentored hundreds of engineers ranging from fresh graduates preparing for their first software engineering role to experienced professionals preparing for senior engineering and architecture interviews.

My teaching philosophy is simple:

Understand why something works before learning how to implement it.

Rather than focusing on memorization, I help engineers develop transferable problem-solving skills that can be applied to unfamiliar challenges across software engineering, system design, and large-scale architecture.

Currently, I work as a Software Developer and Instructor at Scaler, one of India's most rigorous software engineering programs.
